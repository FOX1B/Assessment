Who Are We?

Welcome to Native Wildlife & Environment! This website was created to raise awareness about New Zealand's unique native wildlife and the importance of protecting our natural environment.

Why Is Native Wildlife Important?

New Zealand is home to many unique native species, including the kiwi, kākāpō, tuatara and kea. However, many native animals face threats such as introduced predators, habitat loss and pollution. Learning about these issues is an important step towards protecting our wildlife for future generations.

Our Connection to Kaitiakitanga

Kaitiakitanga is the idea of guardianship and caring for the natural environment. Our website encourages people to respect nature, protect wildlife habitats and make environmentally friendly choices. Everyone can play a part in caring for New Zealand's environment.

Our Mission

Our mission is to help people understand New Zealand's native wildlife and discover simple ways to protect it. We hope our website inspires visitors to care for nature and help preserve our unique environment.
