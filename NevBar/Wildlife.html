A World of Unique Wildlife

Isolated for millions of years, New Zealand developed incredible native species found nowhere else on Earth. However, habitat loss, introduced predators and environmental changes threaten many of these animals. Protecting them is essential for future generations.

Meet Our Native Species

Get to know some of New Zealand's remarkable wildlife.

CRITICALLY ENDANGERED

Kākāpō

The kākāpō is a rare, flightless, nocturnal parrot. It is known for its green feathers and owl-like face. Learn more ↗
NATIVE BIRD

Kiwi

The kiwi is a flightless bird with nostrils near the tip of its long beak. It uses its strong sense of smell to help find food. Learn more ↗
ANCIENT SPECIES

Tuatara

The tuatara is a unique reptile and the only surviving member of an ancient group that lived alongside dinosaurs. Learn more ↗
RARE NATIVE BIRD

Takahē

The takahē is a large, flightless bird with striking blue and green feathers. Conservation programmes have helped its population recover. Learn more ↗

Wildlife Facts

01

Many unique species

New Zealand has many native species that evolved in isolation and are found nowhere else naturally.
02

Flightless birds

Several of New Zealand's native birds, including the kiwi and kākāpō, cannot fly.
03

Conservation matters

Predator control and habitat restoration help protect threatened native wildlife.
